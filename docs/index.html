Format, from the 2025-26 tournaments

  • Six teams qualify. The top two seeds skip the first round. The higher seed hosts each first-round game.

Tiebreakers

The MIAA does not publish its basketball tiebreaker rules. As of October 3, 2026, miaa.org has no handbook, bylaws, sport regulations, or tiebreaker procedure for men's or women's basketball. The conference's Championship Determination page says that the champion is the team with the best MIAA won-loss record. It says that the tournament champion gets the NCAA bid. It does not say how the MIAA orders teams with the same record.

These rules decide seeds, first-round byes, home games, and in a tie for first place the regular-season title. They affect every team, player, and fan in the conference, and the conference should post them where anyone can read them.

Peer Division III leagues do. The College Conference of Illinois and Wisconsin (CCIW), for example, posts its basketball tiebreaker procedures on its public site.

Evidence for each tiebreaker

This order is an inference from the three ties below and the fan posts. The MIAA does not publish it. Each step says how much evidence backs it.

  1. Head-to-head record among the tied teams. Every source lists this first. In all three verified ties the teams split, so it decided nothing.
  2. Record against the other teams, one at a time, from the top of the standings down. Confirmed by the 2024 men's race. Trine and Calvin split, Trine went 2-0 against third-place Hope, and Calvin went 1-1. Trine got the #1 seed even though Calvin had the better second-half record, 6-1 to 5-2. So this step comes before the second-half step.
  3. Road record in conference games. Fan-posted only. In all three verified ties the road records were equal, so no case confirms this step or its place in the order.
  4. Record in the second half of the double round robin. Confirmed by the 2023 and 2024 women's races. Both school releases name it. In both cases the teams split head to head, had identical records against every other team, and had identical road records. That fits a step that sits after the first three.
  5. Coin flip. One fan post names it. No case confirms it.

The evidence does not show how the MIAA breaks a three-team tie. One fan post says the conference once worked up from the bottom of the standings. The evidence also does not show whether the second-half step counts the last half of the schedule or the second meeting with each opponent. The two readings matched in every case here.
