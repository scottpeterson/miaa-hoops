2027 MIAA Tournaments

Checked October 3, 2026. The MIAA has not published 2027 tournament dates, brackets, or hosts. This section gives last season's format and a projection from it.

Format, from the 2025-26 tournaments

  • Six teams qualify. The top two seeds skip the first round. The higher seed hosts each first-round game. The No. 1 seed hosts the semifinals and the final, and keeps hosting the final after a semifinal loss. In 2026 Olivet, the men's top seed, lost its semifinal to Trine, and Hope beat Trine in the final at Olivet the next night.
  • Men: first round on Tuesday, semifinals on Friday, final on Saturday. Women: first round on Wednesday, semifinals on Friday, final on Saturday.
  • The regular-season champion is the team with the best MIAA won-loss record. The tournament champion gets the conference's automatic bid to the NCAA Division III Championship.

Sources: the MIAA's Championship Determination page, 2025-26 championships page, 2026 men's tournament page, and 2026 women's tournament page, plus Olivet's 2026 men's tournament page and Hope's 2026 title game story.

Tiebreakers

The MIAA does not publish its basketball tiebreaker rules. As of October 3, 2026, miaa.org has no handbook, no bylaws, no sport regulations, and no tiebreaker procedure for men's or women's basketball. The conference's Championship Determination page says only that the champion is the team with the best MIAA won-loss record and that the tournament champion gets the NCAA bid. It does not say how the MIAA orders teams with the same record.

These rules decide seeds, first-round byes, home games, and in a tie for first place the regular-season title. They affect every team, player, and fan in the conference, and the conference should post them where anyone can read them. Peer Division III leagues do. The CCIW, for example, posts its basketball tiebreaker procedures on its public site. This page will link to the MIAA's document the day it appears. Until then, the best available evidence is the school releases and fan posts below.

Documents at miaa.net belong to the Mid-America Intercollegiate Athletics Association, a Division II league, and do not apply here.
